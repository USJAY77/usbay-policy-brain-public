==================================================

human_approval_reference

==================================================
PHASE 6 — PILOT READINESS DERIVATION
==================================================

Separate these states:

PLATFORM_READY
PILOT_ONBOARDING_READY
PILOT_ENROLLED
PILOT_RUNTIME_READY
FULL_PRODUCTION_READY

Required semantics:

PLATFORM_READY =
existing production governance checks PASS

PILOT_ONBOARDING_READY =
platform ready
AND enrollment mechanism available
AND fail-closed tests pass

PILOT_ENROLLED =
real customer identity
AND human approval
AND real device/verifier enrollment

PILOT_RUNTIME_READY =
pilot enrolled
AND attestation verified
AND policy valid
AND production sync SYNCED
AND evidence chain valid

FULL_PRODUCTION_READY =
must NOT become true merely because a pilot is enrolled.

Do not collapse these states.
